# Master Prompt: Copy & Paste into Gemini LLM

> **Instructions for the User:**
> 1. Open [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com).
> 2. Attach or copy-paste the markdown files from this `gemini_presentation_kit/` folder.
> 3. Paste the prompt below into the chat box and press enter!

---

### Copy the Prompt Below:

```markdown
You are an elite Silicon Valley venture capitalist, tech product strategist, and presentation designer.

I am providing you with the complete technical and business documentation for "GamerBooker Enterprise v1.0" — a turnkey operating system for modern esports arenas and gaming lounges (featuring a Customer Mobile App with Apple Wallet passes, a Square-style Venue POS Dashboard, a NestJS/PostgreSQL backend engine with mathematical zero-double-booking guarantees, and full Romanian regulatory compliance).

Please review the attached project documentation files:
- 01_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_AND_PITCH.md
- 02_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE_AND_SPECS.md
- 03_PRODUCT_WALKTHROUGH_AND_UI_SCREENS.md
- 04_ROMANIAN_LEGAL_AND_COMPLIANCE_SHIELD.md
- 05_DEV_SETUP_AND_CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md

Based on these documents, please create an extraordinary, professional 12-slide Pitch Deck / Presentation.

For each slide, provide:
1. **Slide Title & Subtitle**
2. **Visual Layout & Graphic Concept** (describe the exact UI mockup, diagram, or graphic to display on the slide)
3. **Key Bullet Points & Content** (high-impact, executive-level language)
4. **Speaker Notes** (what the presenter should say to pitch this slide convincingly to investors, partners, or gaming venue owners)

### Required Slide Structure:
- Slide 1: Cover Slide & Mission Statement
- Slide 2: The Crisis in Gaming Lounge Operations (Lost revenue, double-bookings, manual timers, legal fines)
- Slide 3: The GamerBooker Ecosystem (Mobile App + Venue POS + Core Engine)
- Slide 4: Mathematical Moat: Zero Double-Bookings (PostgreSQL GiST exclusion range locks)
- Slide 5: The Gamer Experience: Apple Wallet 3D Flip Pass & Dynamic Island
- Slide 6: The Seating Minimap & In-Seat Snack Delivery
- Slide 7: The Venue Operations POS: Simple, Tactile & Non-Technical (Square for Gaming)
- Slide 8: The 10-Second Walk-In Customer Intake Flow
- Slide 9: Romanian Regulatory Shield: Law 42/2024 Minors Curfew & ANAF Fiscal Chaining
- Slide 10: Technical Architecture, Performance & Test Verification (525/525 tests passing)
- Slide 11: Business Model, SaaS Subscriptions & Unit Economics
- Slide 12: Go-To-Market Roadmap & Pilot Launch (Nexus Gaming Arena Bucharest)

Make the tone confident, visionary, technically rigorous, and commercial. Output the complete presentation in clean markdown!
```
