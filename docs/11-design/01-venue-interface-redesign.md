# Venue interface redesign — 2026-11-05

The interface uses gaming venue signage and an admission ticket as its visual references. Paper working surfaces (`#F2F0E9`), ink (`#171C21`) and a controlled orange action (`#F2643D`) replace the previous black card grid. Green, amber and red communicate availability, reserved resources and attention separately. Resource identifiers, time and money have strong, tabular hierarchy. Fonts are available system sans faces; no remote font or image loading is required for the redesigned screens.

The dashboard has a fixed dark venue rail with Astăzi, Rezervări, Sala, Comenzi, Încasări and Configurare. Configuration retains the existing layout, pricing and runbook components. The top bar identifies venue and a clearly labelled demo role. Today/Floor combine a restrained summary, shared search/status filtering, room walls, doors and reception/bar wayfinding with a right-hand arrival/attention queue. The plan is an illustrative local schematic, not a claim of surveyed venue geometry. Counts derive from the resources actually displayed. Station details preserve full identifiers/hardware; map nodes use compact names.

Staff scan existing booking codes. Unknown codes are rejected. Walk-in assistance explains the installed-app reservation flow and cannot create bookings (FR-BKG-02). Maintenance cannot replace an active or booked contractual state. Local extension and support demonstrations are labelled; no hardware control or fiscal validation is claimed.

Mobile web and Flutter keep five destinations: Explorează, Rezervă, Permis, Bar, Cont. Explore shows distinct venue rows with intentional floor illustrations, city, price and demo availability. Reserve combines compact start/duration choices, room/station selection, selected equipment details and one persistent total/action. Checkout displays subtotal, deposit discount, due now, remaining balance and final total, with sample policy and explicit payment simulation. Web is a presentation demo, not a parallel production booking channel.

The dark admission ticket puts the station first, followed by venue/time, an explicitly invalid illustrative QR and balances. Extension/support actions remain local demonstrations. Bar uses practical product rows, quantities, cart total and order state. Account prioritizes bookings, documents, preferences, privacy and support. Synthetic XP, tiers and achievements were removed from active screens.

Limitations: this is a local prototype redesign, not production/full client parity. Account subpages are explanatory demo disclosures; real history, receipts and settings are not connected. The old reset-fix/release/signing task remains paused and excluded. Android build/signing settings were not redesigned. Exact verification evidence and remaining checks are in [the UI handoff](../../handoff/HANDOFF-UI-REDESIGN-2026-11-05.md).
